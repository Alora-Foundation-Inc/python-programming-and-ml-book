# Chapter 10 — Snake Game Project (Part 2): Food, Growth, Score

This chapter upgrades your Snake from movement-only to a **real game**:
- Food that appears at random grid positions
- Collision with food
- Body growth (new segments)
- Score (and high score) display
- Speed up as you eat more
- Full Chapter 10 playable Snake

## Files
- `01_food_basic.py` — make a red food circle
- `02_food_random.py` — place food on random grid points
- `03_collision_distance_test.py` — check distance(head, food)
- `04_food_collision_interactive.py` — arrow keys + eat/respawn food
- `05_add_segment_demo.py` — add to `segments` list
- `06_growing_snake_move_demo.py` — moving head + following body
- `07_food_and_growth_integration.py` — eat → grow → respawn food
- `08_score_display_basics.py` — score + high score text pen
- `09_complete_snake_ch10.py` — Chapter 10 full game (reset on wall)
- `10_multi_food_starter.py` — Try-It: multiple food items
- `11_special_food_types_starter.py` — Try-It: food with different effects
- `12_two_player_snake_starter.py` — Try-It: snake vs snake
- `replit/main.py` — quick runner
